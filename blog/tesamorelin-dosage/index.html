Tesamorelin Dosage: Protocol and Chart

Research reference · Updated September 2026 · ~6 min read

Tesamorelin is a stabilized GHRH analog and one of the few peptides in this category with approved clinical use for a specific condition. This guide collects reported dosing into a reference chart. Figures reflect research and clinical literature and are not dosing advice.

How tesamorelin is dosed

Tesamorelin is given by subcutaneous injection and dosed in milligrams. In its approved clinical context it is used at a standard daily amount. Research protocols reference that figure as their anchor.

ContextReported doseFrequency
Clinical reference2 mgOnce daily, subcutaneous
Research protocols1 to 2 mgOnce daily, often at night

Convert a milligram dose into syringe units with the dosage calculator based on your vial size and water volume. Tesamorelin is usually reconstituted fresh and handled with care.

Timing: like other GHRH analogs, tesamorelin is often dosed at night to align with the natural overnight growth hormone pulse and away from food.

How it compares

Tesamorelin is distinct from shorter GHRH peptides because it is stabilized against rapid breakdown. For how it differs from another popular growth hormone peptide, see tesamorelin vs ipamorelin. To understand the mechanism first, read what does tesamorelin do.
