TB-500 Dosage: Research Protocol and Chart

Research reference · Updated September 2026 · ~6 min read

TB-500 is a synthetic peptide based on a region of thymosin beta-4, studied for cell migration and repair. This guide collects the dosing ranges that appear in research protocols into a clear chart. Figures are research-reported and are not dosing advice.

How TB-500 dosing is structured

TB-500 protocols usually run in two phases. A loading phase uses higher weekly amounts split across doses, followed by a maintenance phase at a lower frequency. Doses are given in milligrams, which is larger than the microgram doses used for peptides like BPC-157.

PhaseReported weekly rangeFrequency
Loading (first weeks)4 to 10 mgSplit into 2 doses per week
Maintenance2 to 6 mgOnce weekly or every 2 weeks

A typical research block runs 4 to 6 weeks of loading, then maintenance. To convert a milligram dose into a syringe volume, use the dosage calculator with your vial size and water amount.

Worked example: a 5 mg vial reconstituted with 2.5 ml gives 2000 mcg/ml, which is 2 mg/ml. A 2 mg dose is then 1 ml. A 2.5 mg dose is 1.25 ml.

Why the loading phase exists

TB-500 is often described as slower acting than some peptides, so protocols front-load the first weeks to build up presence, then taper to maintenance. The method notes are on the TB-500 protocol page.

Pairing and accuracy

TB-500 is sometimes researched alongside BPC-157 in recovery protocols. Whatever the setup, measure consistently and review the side effect overview before adjusting.
