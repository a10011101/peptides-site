Step-by-Step Protocol
  1. Allow vial and BAC water to reach room temperature (15–20 min)
  2. Wipe the top of both vials with an alcohol swab and let dry
  3. Draw up the calculated BAC water volume into a sterile syringe
  4. Insert needle into the peptide vial at an angle — inject BAC water slowly down the glass wall (do not spray directly onto the powder)
  5. Gently swirl the vial — do not shake or vortex
  6. Continue swirling until solution is completely clear
  7. Store reconstituted vial in refrigerator (2–8°C), away from light
  8. Mark vial with date — use within 30 days (BAC water) or 7 days (sterile water)
