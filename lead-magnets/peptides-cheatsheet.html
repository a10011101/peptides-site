Reconstitution in 5 steps

  1. Bring the vial and bacteriostatic water to room temperature. Swab both stoppers with alcohol.
  2. Draw your planned volume of bacteriostatic water into a sterile syringe.
  3. Insert the needle at an angle and let the water run slowly down the vial wall, not onto the powder.
  4. Swirl gently until clear. Do not shake.
  5. Label the vial with the date and concentration. Store refrigerated at 2 to 8 C.

The dosing math

Concentration = peptide amount (mcg) ÷ water added (ml).
Volume to draw = target dose (mcg) ÷ concentration (mcg/ml).
On an insulin syringe, 100 units = 1 ml, so 0.1 ml = 10 units.
